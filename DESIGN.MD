# AURA: The Digital Sanctuary

## 1. Vision & Core Philosophy
AURA is designed as a **"Living Sanctuary"** — a calm, intelligent AI companion that transcends the typical clinical or utility-focused assistant. The goal is to provide a peaceful digital environment where users can nurture their growth in health, beauty, fashion, and productivity.

**The Creative North Star:** "Evolve your life, naturally."

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Design Language

### Color Palette: "Nurturing Greens"
- **Primary:** Soft Sage (#8BA888) and Emerald Accents (#4b664a).
- **Secondary:** Warm Beige (#f2f4f2), Pure White, and Light Gray.
- **Rationale:** These tones are inspired by the natural world to reduce digital eye strain and promote a sense of well-being.

### Typography
- **Headlines:** *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Display-LG). Modern, approachable, and confident.
- **Body:** *Manrope*. Highly readable with generous tracking to keep the interface feeling "breathable."

### Shapes & Organic Elements
- **Flowing Curves:** All containers and cards use high corner radius (up to `round-full`) to avoid the rigidity of standard grids.
- **Organic Shapes:** Backgrounds feature soft, fluid blobs and leaf motifs that suggest growth and movement.
- **Depth:** Soft, ambient shadows ("humming" elevations) rather than sharp lines.

---

## 3. User Experience (UX) Strategy

### Atmosphere & Tone
- **Gentle AI:** The tone of voice is supportive, calm, and motivating.
- **Anti-Clutter:** Every screen prioritizes whitespace. If a piece of information doesn't contribute to tranquility, it is secondary.

### Core User Flows
1.  **Onboarding (The Germination):** A multi-step flow that builds a personal "AURA" profile through visual goal-setting and data input.
2.  **Home Dashboard (The Garden):** A daily feed of personalized suggestions (Outfits, Meals, Rituals) presented as easy-to-digest cards.
3.  **Interaction (The Dialogue):** A minimal chat interface that feels like a conversation with a mentor, not a bot.
4.  **Growth Tracking (The Evolution):** Visualizing progress through "Transformation Charts" and "Growth Journeys" (Before/After analysis).

---

## 4. Interaction & Motion Principles
- **Transitions:** Every screen change should feel like a gentle fade or a soft horizontal slide.
- **Micro-interactions:** Buttons respond with tactile, organic "bounces" (0.95x scale on active).
- **Gamification:** Focus on "Streaks" and "Levels" (e.g., Level 4: Sprouting) to encourage daily wellness rituals without creating anxiety.

---

## 5. Screen Inventory
- **Splash Screen:** Immediate immersion into the sanctuary.
- **Onboarding Flow:** Welcome, Data Input, Goals Selection, Profile Creation.
- **Dashboard:** The central hub for "Today's Rituals."
- **Assistant Chat:** Direct access to AURA intelligence.
- **Specialized Insights:** Outfit Recommender, Beauty Analysis, Health & Meals.
- **Operational Tools:** AURA Planner (Schedule), Focus Mode (Pomodoro).
- **Profile:** Comprehensive transformation tracking and achievements.

---

## 6. Technical Foundation
Built using the **Aura Ethereal Design System**, utilizing Tailwind CSS for rapid, consistent styling and Lucide/Google Icons for clean, recognizable iconography.
